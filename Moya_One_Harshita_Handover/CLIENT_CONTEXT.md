# Client context: Moya One

Summary of the client calls on 19 Oct and 22 Oct 2026, with commercial and personal details removed. The client is Omar (also appears as Khan Mohammed).

## The product

- A centralized, subscription-based drinking-water service for residential towers.
- Installed during construction, through the builder. A dedicated water line runs to every flat.
- Each flat has a Moya1 control unit in the kitchen, which controls the flow and measures the water quality for that flat.
- A control room in the basement or cellar monitors the whole system.
- Residents recharge from a phone app, like a mobile top-up.
- No filters or servicing for residents, and no storing or lifting 20-litre bottles.
- The client claims zero water wastage, unlike RO systems. This is not yet verified.
- Deployments: India only, with a Hyderabad community being installed. Details pending.
- UAE: in talks with developers. Do not name them or show their logos.

## The two audiences

1. **Consumers (first on the page).** Message: convenience. No bottles, no filters, no hassle. They need to trust the water, so show per-flat readings (labelled as sample data until confirmed).
2. **Builders and developers (after the consumer sections).** Message: a practical advantage. It's installed at construction stage, so there's no retrofit, and it saves space in every flat.

## What the client asked for

- A brand that feels premium and luxurious, but still convenient. The client compared it to a Mercedes rather than a Maruti.
- An interactive site, not a static template. Builders should find it compelling.
- A home page that serves both audiences, with the consumer sections first.
- A builders section, plus an environment page, a "How we work" page, and a contact page.
- Comparison pages: Moya One vs bottled water vs RO filters. The client wants to speak directly to bottle suppliers and the traditional RO filter market.
- A blog or insights section is deferred until the builder handover is complete.
- No login, no payments and no app features in phase 1. A later phase adds login with a payment ID and OTP.
- A video that explains the whole process. It sits in the builders section, not as the hero.
- The client says the control room should show machinery and pipes, but Vinayak and Rayansh agreed to keep it basic and not reveal technical detail.

## Messaging rules we agreed

- Do not explain the patent or the technology. Overview only.
- Say "patent filed", not "patented".
- Do not claim UAE deployments.
- Every number is labelled as sample data or illustrative.
- No prices on the site in phase 1.
- The website should not sound like a template. Avoid "seamless", "unlock", "empower" and "revolutionary".

## Brand

- Moya One means "water" and "leader", according to the client.
- Logo: the client's team is producing variations. They're AI-generated, and Vinayak is rebuilding them.
- The client has no brand guidelines. We are creating them.
- Client spelling options so far: Moya One, MoyaOne, MoyàOne, Moya1. Not yet confirmed.
