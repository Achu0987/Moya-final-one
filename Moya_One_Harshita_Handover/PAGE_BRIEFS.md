# Page briefs: Moya One website

Each page below states what the page is for, who it's for, and what the visitor should leave with. The content, structure, sections, and interactions are yours to design, within the rules in CLAUDE.md and DESIGN.md.

Rules that apply to every page:
- Consumers come first on the page. Builder content comes after, unless the page is a builder page.
- One main action per view.
- Claims come only from the Claims tab, marked OK or Verified. If a claim is "to confirm", say so in a placeholder, not as fact.
- Numbers are labelled "Sample data" or "Illustrative".
- No prices, no patent or technology detail ("patent filed" only), no UAE developer names.
- If a page needs something this brief doesn't cover, ask Vinayak before building it.

---

## 1. Home (/)

**Essence:** the hassle of bottles and filters is gone, and one pipe, a phone top-up, and trustworthy water replace them. A builder who lands here should see there's a real product behind it.
**Visitor:** a resident who has heard of Moya One, or a builder sent the link.
**Leave with:** "This is simple, and I can trust it."
**Main action:** see how it works.
**Must not say:** anything about the technology or deployments beyond India (marked "to confirm").
**Done when:** a visitor understands the offer in under 60 seconds, and it passes the design audit.

---

## 2. How it works (/b2c/how-it-works)

**Essence:** the full path from the building to the glass, in terms a resident can follow.
**Visitor:** a resident who wants to understand the system before trusting it.
**Leave with:** a clear picture of one line to the flat, a control unit in the kitchen, and recharging from a phone.
**Main action:** check water quality.
**Must not say:** how the unit works inside, or any part names.
**Done when:** a visitor can explain the process back in their own words.

---

## 3. Water quality (/b2c/water-quality)

**Essence:** the resident can see what's in their water, for their flat, any time.
**Visitor:** a resident who is worried about water quality.
**Leave with:** confidence that the water is watched and shown openly.
**Main action:** talk to us.
**Must not say:** that readings are real until the client confirms the parameters and the data source. Use sample data until then.
**Done when:** every reading is labelled, and the parameter list matches what the client has confirmed.

---

## 4. Builders (/b2b/builders)

**Essence:** Moya One is a practical feature a builder should plan in at construction stage: nothing to retrofit, nothing lost from the flats, and an easier sale.
**Visitor:** a builder or developer evaluating options for a project.
**Leave with:** "This is a reason to add it to my project, and they've done it before."
**Main action:** start an enquiry.
**Must not say:** minimum occupancy or guarantee terms, unless the client confirms them in writing. Named projects only after written permission.
**Done when:** the MP4 explainer plays on mobile, and reference projects are clearly marked as placeholders until approved.

---

## 5. Installation (/b2b/installation)

**Essence:** what the installation looks like from the cellar to every flat, without technical detail.
**Visitor:** a builder's technical lead or project manager.
**Leave with:** a clear, basic picture of the cellar, the riser, and the flat.
**Main action:** start an enquiry.
**Must not say:** anything that reveals the technology or internal valves.
**Done when:** the cellar looks clean and evocative, and it passes the design audit.

## 6. Compare (hub) (/compare)

**Essence:** the three options side by side, and why towers switch.
**Visitor:** anyone deciding between Moya One and what they use today.
**Leave with:** Moya One removes the effort and waste of the other two.
**Main action:** go to the bottle or RO comparison.
**Must not say:** any number not confirmed, or any brand name unless the client approves it.
**Done when:** the comparison works on a 390px screen, with no horizontal scroll.
