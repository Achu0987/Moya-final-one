## Product Purpose

Moya One is a centralised, subscription-based drinking-water service for residential towers. A tower gets a dedicated water line to every flat. Each kitchen gets a Moya1 control unit and an extra tap. Residents recharge from a phone app, the way they top up a mobile plan. The system is installed during construction, through the builder.

Success: a resident understands within five seconds that the hassle of jugs and filters is gone; a builder sees a practical reason to install it (no retrofit, no lost space).

## Positioning

Drinking water as a utility built into the tower, recharged in litres like a mobile plan, instead of something carried in or filtered flat by flat.
